Last updated: October 28, 2026

Ovelhinha is a Bible story app for children. It was made to work without asking for any personal information.

Data we collect

None. Ovelhinha does not ask for a name, email address, age, photos, location or any other data. There is no sign-up, no advertising, no tracking and no usage analytics.

What is stored on the device

The app only stores the sound setting, the chosen language, the page where each book was left and which extra stories have been unlocked. This information stays on the device itself, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and can be erased by uninstalling the app (reading progress can also be erased in the Parents’ area).

Purchases

In the app, some stories are free and the others can be unlocked with a one-time purchase made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Payment is made and processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; Ovelhinha never receives your name, email, card details or any other payment data. To know which stories have already been bought, the app asks the device’s own store and keeps that list only on the device. The website sells nothing.

Internet

The stories are already inside the app, which works offline; the internet is only used when buying or restoring purchases, and that connection is made by Apple’s or Google’s store. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any server, it may log technical access data, such as IP addresses, for security purposes, under GitHub’s policy. Ovelhinha has no access to those logs.

Children

The app was designed for children and complies with children’s data protection laws such as COPPA (United States), the GDPR (European Union) and the LGPD (Brazil). Since we collect no data, there is no children’s data to store, sell or share.

Links and purchases

Purchase buttons and links that leave the app only appear after a question for grown-ups, in the Parents’ area or on the locked-story notice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
